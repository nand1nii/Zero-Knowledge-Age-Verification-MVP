The 18+ Lounge

This page is for adults only. Prove you are at least 18 with your Demoland ID card. Your wallet makes a zero-knowledge proof on your own device, so this site never sees your birthdate, name or ID number.

How does this work?
  1. This site asks its server for a fresh, single-use challenge (valid for 2 minutes).
  2. Your wallet opens in a popup with that challenge. You drop in your ID card and approve.
  3. The wallet proves "a trusted issuer signed my birthdate and it makes me 18+" and sends back only the proof.
  4. The server checks every public value and the proof. Then it lets you in.
